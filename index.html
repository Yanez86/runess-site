The story

At the top of the castle's tallest tower, a princess waits for a knight to rescue her, just like in the fairy tales. She waits a long time. Knights set out, but none of them ever reach the top.

One morning she has had enough. She pushes the great door open and starts going down, floor by floor, with a single spell in her hand: the enchanted bolt. She'll find the knights along the way, slumped against the walls, with their promises.

Nobody is coming to save her. She saves herself.

How it plays

A voxel action FPS with procedural floors and a roguelite heart: go down, gather gifts, fall, and start again stronger.

She saves herself

Fast first-person action. No sword, no armour: just the enchanted bolt, a little comet that charges, bounces, burns, freezes and puts to sleep, depending on the gifts you carry.

Every room is a trial

Step in, the doors slam shut and enemies rise from the smoke, wave after wave. They open again only when the last one falls. Spikes, arrow slits, bewitched mirrors: watch your step.

Gifts with a price

124 gifts to collect: they change your bolt, your stride, your . Some give a lot and take something back. Choose carefully what you carry down.

One hand, one pocket

No inventory: one item in your hand, one trinket in your pocket, your and your mana. Everything else you wear, and it shows.

Every descent is new

Rooms, corridors, enemies and treasure all grow from the run's seed: every descent is a different castle, and the same seed builds the same castle again.

A castle full of secrets

Walls to knock on, golden rooms behind locked doors, doors that shouldn't be there. And a story you only uncover by going down.

The castle

Below the tower, the castle changes its face with every descent: each floor has its own place, with its own monsters, lights and guardians.

The Cellars

Stone and packed earth, chains and banners, bones in the corners: the castle as it once was, underneath.

Home to: Goblin, Skeleton Archer, Giant Rat, Ghoul

The Noble Halls

Parquet, chequered marble, red wallpaper and portraits that watch you go by. More light, but no fewer dangers.

Home to: Goblin, Skeleton Warrior, Flying Chicken, Wraith

The Caves

Raw rock and stalactites, pools of water, crystals and little mushrooms glowing green. No windows: only lanterns set on the ground.

Home to: Wolf, Giant Spider, Giant Rat, Worm

The Wizard's Tower

A blue vault full of stars, glowing rune circles, bookshelves, alembics and crystal balls: here the rooms cast spells of their own.

Home to: Fire Elemental, Flying Grimoire, Ice Elemental, Wraith

The Castle Dungeons

Wet cobblestones, barred cells, chains and rotten straw. Dark, and without a single window.

Home to: Skeleton Warrior, Zombie, Skeleton Archer, Giant Rat

And further down?

The castle goes deeper than this. What's down there, you'll find out on the way.

Bestiary

Fairy-tale monsters built from voxels, jointed and animated, each with its own way of moving and striking. There are 53 in the game: these are the first you'll meet.

A guardian before every staircase

Trolls, ettins, fire mages, kings of bone: every guardian has its phases, its combos and a ring of light on the floor that tells you where it's about to strike. Read it, dodge, strike back.

Gifts

124 gifts, each with its own fairy tale and its own icon. Every gift that touches your bolt leaves its mark on it, and many show on the princess herself.

  • simple gifts
  • medium gifts
  • major gifts

And further down, gifts best left unnamed.

Between descents

Every descent ends back in the tower, and the tower remembers.

Promises and keepsakes

Knights and monsters leave promises behind. Back in the tower you write them on your little board, and they become keepsakes: things for your room that help you on the next descent.

Spells

Instead of a jump you have a spell: the blink, to begin with. The other 6 are learned by following the fairy tales, going down and studying in the tower.

Tomorrow's tale

The princess's diary lies on the lectern. Write a page of tomorrow's tale, and the floor you're about to go down to will turn out the way it says.
