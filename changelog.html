0.12.1latest17 October 2026 · 14 changes

Additions

  • A new keepsake, after the sixth-floor guardian: from then on trinkets turn up in vases, crates and shops.

Changes

  • The HUD has been redone: courage is a star next to the mana, timed effects are badges by the minimap, item, spell and pocket have their own slots, and the icons are new.
  • The pause menu has been redone in the HUD's style, with details for everything and a menu to quit.
  • The title screen has been redone: «Continue» resumes your last princess, and each princess has her own card.
  • The television of past descents now stands in the altar room, with a sofa and a rug; the room and its corridor are tidy.
  • Exploding enemies show where they'll blow on the floor, like the guardians' attacks.
  • Achievements, timed gifts and gift messages are shown more cleanly.
  • E takes what you're looking at, not just the nearest thing, and never through a wall.
  • Seven keepsakes have a single grade: buying them all now takes 847 promises.

Fixes

  • Swooping enemies no longer stick in front of your face.
  • Altars rising from the floor no longer come up under your feet or block the way.
  • Enemies that burst from the floor or reappear no longer leave you stuck inside them.
  • «Get unstuck» only puts you where there really is room.
  • The television and the updates no longer spill out of their frames.
0.12.015 October 2026 · 20 changes

New features

  • Trinkets: pocket knick-knacks you use only once, and nobody tells you what they do.
  • A new keepsake that replays all your past descents, how they ended and your records.
  • The princess has a body: you see it looking down, in her shadow and in the pause menu, and many gifts show on her.
  • In the pause menu, «Get unstuck» if you get stuck, and «Report a problem», which sends a report with a screenshot.
  • A comic book to collect.

Additions

  • The guardians of the first floors have their own combos.
  • Many more vases and crates to break, in lots of new designs.
  • The castle is more furnished: rugs, hanging things, furniture against the walls, columns and arches in the big rooms.
  • One more secret, for those who have reached the end of the story.

Changes

  • The enchanted bolt is a little comet, and every gift that touches it leaves its mark.
  • Close-range enemies are no longer pinned down by your bolts: you have to dodge.
  • On the first three floors guardians have more health, and frost, sleep and hexes last less on them.
  • The fountain room is a crossroads to different rooms of the floor.
  • Items on the minimap come with a keepsake, and some keepsakes change their grades.
  • The stars of a constellation want different guardians.
  • The encyclopedia has been redone and opens without freezing the game.

Fixes

  • The game no longer freezes for a few seconds when a big guardian appears.
  • Furniture no longer sinks into the walls, and traps and trapdoors no longer flicker.
  • Big enemies no longer get stuck in doorways, and arena traps no longer cover trapdoors.
  • In the pause menu, after a click on nothing, arrows and the controller start again from the button you were on.
0.11.013 October 2026 · 24 changes

New features

  • Music arrives: a theme for the menu, tracks that start now and then on the way down, and a track for the story's battles.
  • Companions: gifts that follow you through the castle, one at a time, each helping in its own way.
  • The completion percentage, on the save slot and in the achievements.
  • The story is split into acts, announced when you wake up.

Additions

  • Two new guardians.
  • New sounds: entering the game, raising a shield, at dawn, picking up potions and gifts, and your heartbeat when you're nearly done for.
  • New achievements, one per constellation and one per area; a few have been removed.
  • New secrets at the bottom of the castle.

Changes

  • Guardians are animated piece by piece, and monsters' eyes glow in the dark.
  • Every guardian shows on the floor where it's about to strike, in the colour of the attack; if you're inside it you notice it from the edge of the screen and a hum too.
  • Heavy blows shake the view (it can be turned off); volumetric fog has been removed.
  • Promises in your pocket stay if you die, but unspent ones are lost when you go through the great door.
  • A fallen guardian leaves a random gift, not always a major one.
  • Constellations are earned one star at a time, and in the planetarium the slots are frames.
  • The board only shows what you've bought and what you can buy now.
  • Settings only apply with the Apply button.
  • In the diary you only write on the floor you're going down to, and the chart between floors reaches only that far.
  • Objects for spells only turn up after the magic tome.
  • Two steps of the story arrive in a different way, for you to discover.
  • The mouse pointer is a magic wand.
  • With a controller the talent tree is walked the way it reads.

Fixes

  • The summary at the great door and the pause menu no longer spill off the screen.
  • In the diary, mouse and controller no longer take the pointer away from each other.
  • Esc while waiting for a new key just cancels.

0.10.19 October 2026 · 17 changes

New features

  • Courage and talents: levelling up lights the stars of constellations, chosen by looking at the sky from the princess's room.
  • Tomorrow's tale: in the diary you write how a floor you can see will go, and every page has its price.
  • Spells instead of the blink: six more to learn, one in hand at a time.
  • The spell study, and the maze-like library where the magic tome is found.

Additions

  • A new keepsake that marks on the minimap what there is to pick up.
  • Every gift has its own pixel-art icon, and a spinning medallion on the altars.
  • A new page of tomorrow's tale.

Changes

  • Every lit talent, like gifts, gives enemies a little more health.
  • Some gifts and keepsakes do something else, and those that affected the blink now work for any spell.
  • The pause menu is bigger, split into tabs, opens on talents, and has «Back to title».
  • The shield sits at the bottom with its state, sun and moon under the minimap, and loading has its own hourglass.
  • The smoke enemies come out of tells you who's coming.
  • When you create a slot you no longer choose the easy game.
  • From floor 4, arenas leave a gift a little less often.

Fixes

  • With a controller, the settings always show where you are.

0.1.027 September 2026 · 13 changes

New features

  • Runess is born from Dungeon nell'oscurità: same voxel graphics, same procedural floors, a new game.
  • The enchanted bolt and mana.
  • Arena rooms: you step in, runes seal the exits, and they only open again when the last enemy falls.
  • Relics: a gift for every cleared arena.
  • Day and night, with windows looking outside.
  • Slumped knights with their promises, the shop and the golden doors.

Additions

  • Eleven gifts.
  • A new potion.

Changes

  • No inventory: a single item in your hand.
  • The castle is lit: no more darkness to fear.
  • Enemies attack as soon as you walk in and always know where you are.
  • The sword, the torches to light, the treasures, the backpack and some traps are gone.
  • You can always climb out of trapdoors by the rope.
