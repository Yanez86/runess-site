0.12.1latest17 October 2026 · 18 changes
  • Trinkets are no longer around from the start: they come with a new keepsake, the Treasure Jar, which appears on the board the first time you defeat the guardian of the sixth floor. Once bought (12 promises) it sits on a stool to the right of the bed, and from then on trinkets turn up in vases, crates and shops. If you have already beaten the sixth floor, it is already on your board.
  • The goblin bomber now warns of its blast like the guardians do: a red ring on the floor with a curtain of light rising from it, growing stronger as the blast draws near, and your hand and the edge of the screen light up if you are inside. The same goes for the gingerbread man and the kettle.
  • The Old Television is no longer in your bedroom: it now stands in the altar room, against the wall you come in through, on the side of the toy table, and it comes with a green velvet sofa, a crocheted blanket over its back, and an oval braided rug. And the altar room is now as tidy as the bedroom: no worn parquet, bones, rubble or cobwebs, and the same goes for the corridor that leads there.
  • The HUD has been redone. Courage is a star at the end of the mana bar that fills with gold from the bottom, with your level beside it. When you level up, a banner at the top tells you how many stars there are to light, then its star flies next to your courage and stays there with the pause key until you light them; behind closed doors, when stars can't be lit, it turns grey with a padlock and lights up again as soon as the doors open. At the bottom there is no more box: the item in your hand sits in a slot, the spell in a bigger round frame, with its charges as gems on the rim and the recharge sweeping round like a clock hand, the trinket in a stitched pocket, and the keys on the rim. The shield sits next to your hearts, one little shield for each hit it can still take; timed effects are badges at the top right, to the left of the minimap: potions still working, with a border that wears away, and the curse of the cursed chest, a purple skull showing how much more health enemies have. Potions, spells, trinkets, promises and keys have new icons, drawn in pixel art like those of the gifts.
  • The pause menu has been redone too, in the style of the new HUD: it sits in the middle of the screen and dims everything behind it, and every tab is laid out the same way, with its title, its summary on the right, the list below and on the right the detail of whatever you point at, with the mouse or the controller. In the Descent tab you find what you carry just as in the HUD: hearts, mana and courage, the item, the spell and the trinket, the potions still working, the curse of the chest and all your gifts, each with its detail. In Stats the detail tells you what changes each number, with icons; in Achievements there is the big medal. At the bottom are the floor and the version, and «Quit» opens a menu with «Restart the descent», which asks you to press twice, «Back to title» and «Quit game». The banner of an achievement you reach waits until you resume.
  • The fairy godmother's gift no longer sits in a box of its own: it is a badge next to the minimap, with the potions, its icon under a veil that closes like a clock hand and tells you how long until the toll that will make it fade.
  • The banner of an achievement you reach no longer appears at the top centre: it appears at the bottom right, stays a few seconds, then slides down past the edge of the screen and disappears.
  • Taking a gift no longer shows a message repeating what it does: its card already tells you, before you take it. A message remains only when there is something more to know: that it took the place of your previous bolt shape or companion, that it is asleep for now, or that there is no room for another .
  • Flying chickens and everything that swoops down like them (bats, magpies, gargoyles, swan geese, wyverns, flying spindles) no longer stay glued in front of your face: their dive goes right through you and they come out the other side.
  • Altars rising from the floor, with a guardian's gift or the gift of a cleared arena, no longer rise under your feet or block your way: in a small guardian room the altar could rise in front of the passage to the stairs and leave the floor with no way out, and in an arena with a single door it could rise in front of the door and shut you in.
  • The monster under the bed and the other guardians that burst out from under the floor no longer leave you stuck inside them when they come out right beneath your feet: they move aside and you walk out. And the imp no longer reappears on top of you, nor leaves an invisible body in your way if it falls while vanished in its sulphur.
  • «Get unstuck» only puts you back where you really fit, never inside something standing in the middle of the room.
  • E takes or uses what you are looking at: with a potion on the floor nearby and a gift in front of you, you take the gift, without having to walk away from the potion. Between two things in reach, the one under your crosshair wins, then the one nearest the middle of your view; only if there is nothing in front of you, the nearest one. And things behind a wall can no longer be taken.
  • Seven keepsakes now have a single grade: the Porcelain Piggy Bank, the Music Box, the Cup of Chamomile, the Seven Dwarfs' Beds, the Knight's Helm, the Seven-League Boots and the Jewelry Box. They do what they did at their first grade, and the Seven Dwarfs' Beds no longer make you find less courage. Buying every keepsake at every grade now takes 847 promises.
  • The title screen has been redone in the style of the pause menu: it grows with the window, and the title and menu sit on the left, so the princess's room shows in full. «Continue» takes you straight back to your last princess and tells you who she is and how far she got. «The princesses» has a card for each slot, with her name and kingdom, floor, act, how much of the game you have done, days in the tower, promises, keepsakes, escapes, deaths and time played, and the room behind becomes hers; to delete one there is the bin on the card, or Delete (X on the controller), and the question appears inside the card. At the top right «What's new», tagged «new» until you have read it and listing every version, and «Wiki», which warns you about spoilers right under its button.
  • With many descents the Old Television's channel list no longer spills below the screen: it stays inside and scrolls.
  • In the Old Television the route of a long descent no longer shrinks until you cannot tell the floors apart: it wraps onto several rows, with an arrow leading back to the left, and the boxes stay big with the floor number below.
  • In the updates, versions with a name (like 0.7.0, Balance update) show it next to the number without spilling into the left column any more; the date is in the title on the right.
0.12.015 October 2026 · 30 changes
  • The magic bolt is no longer a little blue dot: it is a small comet, with a head of blue cubes that tumbles and trembles with energy, a ringed halo of light around it and a trail that grows longer the faster it flies. Fire, frost and sleep bolts are comets of their own colours; at night, with the Veil of Night, an eclipse with a black heart.
  • Every gift that touches the bolt leaves its mark on it, minor gifts a little and major ones a lot: Tailwind two wisps of wind at its sides, Twin Bolt a little star circling it, String of Pearls one pearl per copy, the Spyglass the brass rim of a lens, the Wax Candle drops of wax, the Blue Rose blue petals, the Crystal Lens crystal shards, the Huntsman's Dagger a red thread in the trail, the Silver Needle a needle ahead of its head and the Hatpin a pin stuck through it; the Huntsman's Bow turns it into an arrow of light with fletching, the Sea King's Trident into a trail of water with three prongs, the Queen's Scepter puts a golden crown around it, the King's Sword a little silver sword, the Brave Little Tailor's Belt seven dots that light up one for each enemy you kill, the Crystal Ball a shell of violet glass; the Rack stretches it, the Thumbscrew squashes it, the Breaking Wheel spins wooden spokes around it, and with the Pillory it is big when you stand still and small when you walk. The more damage it deals, the whiter its heart and the bigger its halo; the faster it flies, the more it stretches forward; and the likelier a critical hit, the more often its heart flashes like a star.
  • In the biome achievements the list of monsters to kill sits in a folded drop-down: it opens with a click, or with the controller, and stays open until you close it.
  • The game no longer freezes for a few seconds when you step onto the platform of the Princess of the Chasm: her model is now prepared while the floor loads, like every boss's.
  • The princess has a body: looking down you see her blue dress, her belt and her boots, and in torchlight her shadow, with her little crown, her braid and her cape. In the pause menu, in the Descent tab, there is her figure: you can turn her by dragging with the mouse or with the right stick. Many gifts show on her: the Sun Crown and the other crowns take the place of her little crown, the Starry Cloak and the other cloaks the place of her cape, Rapunzel's Braid reaches the floor, the Red Shoes colour her boots, and many more.
  • Early-floor bosses have their own combos: attacks made so that the first one pushes you right where the second one lands. When one starts, the boss's eyes flash twice, and the attacks after the first come faster. The giant serpent dives into the ground and races towards you faster than you can run, bursts out beneath your feet and right away its boils spray pus all around, in a wide circle; the fire mage vanishes, reappears at your side and throws a quick fireball, leaving behind an ember that explodes; the troll belches as soon as it lands from its leap; the ettin charges you right after its two clubs, and if it ends up against a wall it is left stunned; the monster under the bed bursts out beneath you, drags you in and claws you. And the bog troll's belch leaves a bog cloud on the ground: while it stands inside, its wounds close even while you hit it.
  • On the first three floors bosses have more health: they went down too quickly.
  • The stars of the Spell constellation only appear once you have the spell study with the magic tome, and each one wants a different guardian: beating the same one again shows no new star, and the ones beaten before do not count.
  • Without the Toy Castle the map no longer shows the items to collect, only the rooms; with the castle, which now has a single grade, the items you have seen and not collected yet appear.
  • The Princess's Bed has a single grade: +1 max . Baby Bear's Bed has two: you choose your starting gift from four, then from five.
  • In the princess's room the furniture no longer sinks into the walls: the headboards of the Princess's Bed and Baby Bear's Bed, the bookcase, the lectern, the mirror stand, the spell study and the keepsakes on their pedestals stand clear of the panelling and the paintings, and the torture table no longer runs into the corner pillars. And open trapdoors no longer flicker: their doors hang inside the pit instead of inside its walls.
  • The pause menu has «Get unstuck»: if you get stuck in a wall, between two pieces of furniture or under the floor, press it twice and you are back at the entrance of the floor (during a fight, with the doors shut, in the middle of the room). You can use it once a minute.
  • The pause menu also has «Report a problem» (or F8 while playing): write what happened and the report reaches the developer with a screenshot of that moment and what is needed to track the problem down (version, seed, floor, where you are, your saves). If it cannot be sent, it stays in a folder you can open from the game and send by hand.
  • The encyclopedia no longer freezes the game when you open it, and it has been redone: on the left the list (enemies and items by name, gifts as small tiles with their icon), on the right the entry you pick, with the monster or item turning, or the gift's card with its flavour text. Models load only when you pick them; the biggest ones show the hourglass for a moment.
  • Traps no longer flicker against walls and ceilings: brambles, the cage and its chains, the grates, the dragon heads with their scorch marks, the arena arrow slits and the pendulums.
  • An arena trap (spikes, tombstones, cracked slabs, mirrors) no longer ends up on top of a trapdoor or another floor trap: in the Cathedral a tombstone could cover the pit.
  • In the pause menu, after a mouse click where there is nothing to press, the arrow keys and the controller's d-pad pick up again from the button you were on, instead of moving nothing at all.
  • Frost, hex, sleep and darkness affect bosses less: they wear off sooner, a new bolt does not extend them, and once they wear off the boss shakes them off for a few seconds. With the Snowflake, bosses no longer stay frozen for the whole first phase.
  • The "Free" achievement is now called "You'll Never Be Free".
  • The bigger enemies (spiders, flying chickens, wyverns, gargoyles…) no longer get stuck in doorways: while chasing you they could stop right in the middle and block an open door, even the black door when, with the boss defeated, you wanted to go back.
  • A new keepsake on the board: the Old Television, for 5 promises. It sits in your room on a little table, with a doily on top and rabbit-ear antennas, and you can move it like the other furniture. Switched on, it shows all your descents again, one per channel (LB and RB, or Page Up and Page Down, jump ten at a time): the day, how it ended and who killed you, your path floor by floor with the guardians you faced, the time, enemies, hits taken, promises, courage, spell, gifts and seed; channel 00 holds your records and whatever killed you most often. Descents are recorded from this version on, even before you buy it.
  • The fountain room is a crossroads: each of its azure doors leads to a different room of the floor, instead of to corridors that looped around it and ran into each other. That is why it sometimes has only three doors, or two.
  • Enemies that attack up close no longer stay pinned down by your bolts: the first hit still makes them stagger and step back, but for a while afterwards your hits only wound and slow them, and once they start winding up an attack, or leap at you, they no longer stop. To keep them away you have to dodge, or use a spell that pushes them back.
  • One more secret, for those who have already reached the end of the story.
  • Rooms now have many more vases and crates to smash, in little groups in the corners and along the walls, in lots of new models: the tall amphora, the big jar, the vase of flowers, the jug, the labelled crate, the long one, stacked crates, the crate full of straw; and some environments have their own: porcelain in the Noble Halls, rough clay pots in the Caves, potion jars in the Wizard's Tower, the black urn in Hell, the marble urn in the Cathedral, the toy box in the Maiden's Dream, crates of vegetables and of bottles. The extra ones are empty: what you find on a floor is the same as before. The «Shards» achievement now asks for 150 vases, and empty ones count too.
  • Trinkets are here: pocket knick-knacks you use only once, and they do strange things. They do not go in your hand but in your pocket, one at a time, and you use them with F (LB on the controller; you can change the key in the settings): the pocket slot appears at the bottom, next to the shield, only when you have one. Each one always does the same thing, but nobody tells you what: you have to try it. The encyclopedia counts them among the items. You find them inside vases and crates, and sometimes the shop sells one instead of the potion.
  • The castle is better furnished. On the floor, rugs (in the noble rooms, in the wizard's tower with its magic circle, in the sleeping castle, in Baba Yaga's house, and the red runner in the Cathedral), open books and scrolls, straw, bowls, drain grates, cold, toppled lanterns, church candles, burst flour sacks, jars of mushrooms; hanging from the ceiling, cages with their prisoner, chains swaying gently and, in the kitchens, meat on hooks; against the walls, shelves of jars with things floating in spirit, empty suits of armour, stopped grandfather clocks, tea tables, globes, anatomy skeletons, hourglasses, confessionals, broken pews, mine carts, stalagmites, the ribcage of an enormous beast, meat racks, chopping blocks with a cleaver, empty cradles and scarecrows. In the walls, niches with a bust and animal hides stretched out to dry, and in the sleeping castle the tables are still laid under the dust, cake and all.
  • Large rooms have columns, in two rows: stone in the dungeons, marble in the noble rooms, rock in the caves, basalt with lava cracks in Hell, clustered shafts reaching the vault in the Cathedral, birch trunks in Baba Yaga's house, smoke-blackened posts in the kitchens, wrapped in brambles in the sleeping castle. They give cover: bolts stop against them, yours and the enemies'. Along the long walls half arches hold up the ceiling, there is an arch over the mouths of corridors, and the kitchens and Baba Yaga's house have wooden beams.
  • Every now and then, tossed in a corner, there is something that should not be in a castle.
  • In secret rooms and golden rooms you may sometimes find a comic book: «Princess Thunderbolt». The issues you find stay on the bookcase in your room, to leaf through.
0.11.013 October 2026 · 35 changes
  • Music arrives. The main menu has a theme of its own; on the way down, every now and then, after a stretch of silence, one of three tracks starts playing; the story bosses come with a battle theme; and outside the castle, the birds sing. Music volume and the music switch are in the Audio settings, the composers in the end credits.
  • New sounds: when you pick a save slot and enter the game, an amulet lighting up ("Epic Amulet Item" by CosmicD), when you strap on a shield ("Item Handling" by qubodup), at dawn, a distant bell ("Church Bell" by Ulrich Metzner; in the cathedral its own bells ring), when you are down to 1 or less, your heart beating until you heal ("Heartbeat Sounds" by bart), and when you cast the orb, your usual bolt ("Magic Smite" by spookymodem).
  • Picking up a potion and taking a gift now have sounds of their own ("100 CC0 SFX" by rubberduck and "Spell Sounds Starter Pack" by p0ss).
  • The story is divided into acts: when one begins, before you wake up, its title appears on a black screen.
  • Companions: twelve new major gifts that follow you through the castle, one at a time. Six come from fairy tales: the Marquis of Carabas's cat, which scratches sleeping enemies and bows at the shop; the talking cricket, which chirps before traps and boss blows, but leaves if you take a wicked gift; Cinderella's doves, which bring you promises and hearts; the frog of the well, which snatches shots out of the air; the seven ravens, which block one blow each but stop your healing at half; the golden goose, which enemies get stuck to. Six more arrive later on, and they come from within. When you take a new one, the old one stays on the ground: you can go back for it, until you take the stairs. Each one is its own little voxel model that follows you a metre and a half behind, walking, hopping or flying, and when you stop it stays put and turns to look at you, so you can turn around and watch it: the cat has a feathered hat, boots and a rapier and now and then licks its paw, the cricket has a top hat and an umbrella, the frog wears a little crown and catches shots with its tongue, the golden goose honks with its wings spread, and at night the doves sleep on the ground with their wings folded.
  • The old bosses rebuilt in pieces and animated, like the enemies: the troll and the bog troll walk with heavy steps, raise their club over their head gnashing their teeth before bringing it down, swing their arm across for the backhand, puff up with arms and mouth wide open for the belch, and when they roar into a new phase they raise the club and beat their chest. The ettin raises both clubs and brings them down one after the other, lifts the boulder with both arms, and its two heads move on their own: now and then they turn to look at each other and grumble, and when it roars they yell one this way and one that way. The king and Dad carry the shield on their arm and the sword in their fist: the shield in front of the chest, centred for the charge, slammed forward; the sword raised over the head before the slash and up to the sky when they roar; the king's cloak sways. The queen and Mum move their hands with the spell: up to the sky for the hex and the ice rain, joined in front of the chest for the darts, one hand forward with each dart, open towards the floor for the frost. The fire mage holds his staff in his right hand and points it or raises it over his head, while his left hand conjures the fire, trembling, and hurls it, and the tip of his hat wobbles. The Skeleton King grips his greatsword with both hands over his head before bringing it down, plants it in the floor while he raises his left hand to call his warriors, and lunges with his arm outstretched; now and then his jaw clatters and his tattered cloak sways. The crystal golem pulls its fist back before striking, raises both fists over its head to pound the floor, and hugs its heart with both arms before the ring of shards, then throws them wide. Fire-Eater holds his whip and you can see it: as he winds up it drags on the floor behind him, then it cracks in a fan in front of him; for the whirling whip it spins over his head and then all around, taut. For the puppets he pulls the strings like a puppeteer, for the sneeze he raises a hand to his nose, stunned he sways, and his floor-length beard swings. The devil draws his pitchfork back before thrusting it, raises it to the sky for the pillars of fire, beckons the imps with a finger and yawns before his nap; his wings breathe, fold back for the charge, spread wide and then beat forward for the wing blast, and his tail wags. The spider of oblivion walks on its eight legs, under the glass it moves them slowly as if swimming, raises its front legs before stabbing and before biting, lifts its abdomen for the webs and rears up when it roars. The lady with the skirt of snakes has a skirt that writhes on its own, and her two snake heads now and then turn to look at each other, draw back before spitting venom or striking and then lunge forward. The monsters' eyes glow in the dark, and so do the gems of the king's crown and the queen's diadem.
  • Bosses feel more alive: their eyes glow brighter and brighter as they wind up a blow, your view shakes when a club, a fist or a leap hits the floor (more when you are close), and when they roar into a new phase they raise the dust and push back anyone standing next to them.
  • In the video settings, among the screen effects, screen shake can be turned off: no shaking when blows hit the ground, not even in the dragon scene.
  • Volumetric fog removed, from the video settings too.
  • Every boss now shows where it is about to strike, like the princess of the abyss: a glowing ring on the floor with a curtain of light rising from it, brighter and brighter as the blow comes, and a flash when it lands. Slashes, club blows and whip lashes have their own warning too, a fan in front of the boss, and charges a strip up to the wall. The colour tells you what is coming: red for blows, orange for fire, light blue for ice, green for poison, purple for magic, gold for Rumpelstiltskin's thread.
  • When you are standing inside a blow's warning you notice it even without looking at the floor: a light in its colour rises from the bottom edge of the screen, your hand lights up from below, and you hear a hum that rises in pitch as the blow comes.
  • Promises in your pocket are still kept when you die, but they must be spent in the tower: when you go through the great door of the descent, any you haven't spent are lost. The summary at the great door warns you if you still have some.
  • The whiteboard only shows what you have already bought and what you can buy right now.
  • Settings take effect with the Apply button: nothing changes until you press it, and if you leave with unapplied changes the game asks whether to apply them or discard them.
  • In the pause menu, Esc while you are changing a key just cancels, without closing the pause menu.
  • The mouse pointer is a magic wand, with a golden star at its tip.
  • New constellations are earned one star at a time: every time you defeat their guardian, or repeat their feat, one more of their stars shows from the French window, with a faint line toward the next. Until it has all six stars a constellation is just a nameless drawing, and you can't choose it.
  • In the telescope's planetarium the spots are boxes, each with the drawing of its constellation: the chosen one turns gold, with a little arrow above it. On the left there is also the Pole Star's box, with a padlock: it is always there and can't be changed. With a controller, A on a box takes you up to the sky, A on a constellation puts it there and brings you back down, B goes back to the boxes.
  • In the pause menu, with a controller you move through the talent stars the way the tree reads: up and down along the steps, left and right between the two branches of a fork and on to the next constellation, LT and RT from one constellation to another. Going up from the first row no longer opens another tab by mistake.
  • When the boss falls it no longer always leaves a major gift: the altar holds a random gift, yellow about one time in eight, otherwise green or blue (blue more often than in the arenas). The diary page "The Giant Woke Up Grumpy" also leaves two random gifts, no longer two major ones.
  • The fairy-tale objects of the spells only turn up on the way down once you have found the magic tome.
  • The puffball and the porcelain doll are a little bigger, so they are easier to spot from a distance.
  • In the diary you can only write on the floor you're going down to: the other floors you can see, under "Further ahead", are only there to look at.
  • Between floors, the descent chart stops at the floor you're going down to: the floors ahead can only be seen in the diary (and there the Magic Mirror shows more of them).
  • In the diary you point with the mouse pointer; with the controller or the arrow keys the focus frame and the little hand next to the page come back.
  • In the diary the wise owl's pages are now called "dictated by the owl", and pointing at one reminds you that it doesn't take a spot.
  • In the long castle Dad no longer waits for ten won descents after the king falls: he comes another way, yours to find out.
  • As long as the dream still holds something for you, if in a descent you don't reach the floor with the pink door, the next descent is sure to have one.
  • New secrets, at the bottom of the castle.
  • New boss in the wizard's tower and the castle prisons: the witch on her broom. She throws her frogs at you, and on the ground they leap at you, croaking; then she vanishes in a puff of green smoke, comes back far away cackling and charges on her broom along the trail that lights up on the ground. If she misses and hits a wall she is dazed, and your hits hurt her more.
  • New boss in the caves, the dungeons and elsewhere: the giant serpent. It lives underground, where nothing can touch it, and you only see the earth moving where it goes; it bursts out of the circle that lights up at your feet. Above ground it shoots poisonous pus from the boils on its body, which leaves puddles that slow and poison you, and it slams its head on the ground. The boils are its weak spot: hit them and they burst, hurt it more and stop spitting, but they grow back at every phase.
  • Achievements reworked: «Halfway There», «Second Star to the Right» and «Giant Slayer» are gone. New ones: one for each constellation you see whole from the French window, and one for each area, «Ruler of…», where you must kill all its monsters, guardians included: below it, a checklist of the ones already fallen, and guardians you have not met yet are just called «Boss». And a few secret achievements further along the story.
  • Completion percentage: on the slot square, next to the name, and at the top of the Achievements tab. It counts the story, the encyclopedia, achievements, keepsakes, the spells you have learned and the stars seen from the French window; at 100% you really have done everything.
  • The summary at the descent door no longer runs off the screen the first time you open it, when it warns you about the gift still on the altar or the promises in your pocket; the same goes for the pause menu.
  • In the diary, with the controller in hand, brushing the mouse no longer removes the frame and the little hand leaving you without a pointer; and the pointer comes back even when you move the mouse slowly.
0.10.310 October 2026 · 27 changes
  • The retro PS1 filter is gone (and the F4 key with it). In its place, in the video settings, screen effects you can turn on one by one: outlines, which sharpen silhouettes and cube edges; area colors, each area with its own tints (blue in the caves, red in hell, pink in the dream); volumetric fog, where torches and lava glow in the air; the softer colors of AgX; and aberration on hits, which briefly splits the colors at the edges of the screen when you get hit. Outlines, colors and aberration start on.
  • Goblins, wolves and bats move in pieces: as they walk their legs and arms move, the bat flaps its wings, the wolf wags its tail, pants and leaps with its jaws wide open, the goblin raises its spear behind its head before throwing it. When they die they collapse with limp limbs, and the wolf falls on its side.
